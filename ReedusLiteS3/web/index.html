          Pair a Lite

          1. Turn your Lite on. Once it's online its face flashes PAIR, then shows 6 digits in two rows.
          2. Type the top row, then the bottom row.

          Teach a voice

          1. Stand where you'd normally talk to him, a few feet away.
          2. Tap Start. He'll say he's listening, then talk for about 20 seconds in your normal voice.
          3. Only one person talks while he learns.
